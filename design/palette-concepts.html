STRIDEKEEP · DESIGN EXPLORATION · 2026-04-20

Four palettes for a tavern, a forge, a scriptorium, a workshop.

Each is faithful to the SKILL.md brief — medieval artisan, dignified working-class, never casino. They diverge in which kind of artisan: candlelit room of a tavern keeper, smoke-and-iron of a forge, vellum-and-gold of a monastic scriptorium, or slate-and-brass of a Victorian workshop. Hover any swatch row to read hex codes.

01 · ANCHOR
Candlelight Tavern
Refined version of the current direction. Warm espresso pooling around firelit cream, brass that catches highlights, brick-red CTAs, guild-indigo for Team Blue. The palette of someone telling stories at a long oak table.

Monastic Vellum

04 · COOLER, MODERN
Slate & Brass
Cool blue-slate base with warm brass accents — a Victorian instrument-maker's bench. Subtle backdrop blur on cards, hairline brass borders, restrained gradients. Reads more contemporary than the others while staying material-honest. Best fit if the audience skews toward warehouse-tech / fulfillment-center workers who use modern apps daily.
