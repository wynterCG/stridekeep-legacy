Stride.keep.

Walk in the real world. Paint a shared mural together.

Coming soon to Android

What it isA walking game with a shared canvas.

Every season, two teams — Red and Blue — paint the same pixel-art mural in parallel. Your real-world step count earns coloured tiles. You place tiles on your team's canvas. The first team to fill the mural wins the season and a permanent badge.

No subscriptions. No real-money purchases. No advertising. Just walking, slowly turning into art.

How it worksThree steps, one season at a time.

Step 1

Walk

Stridekeep reads your daily steps from Google Health Connect. The more you walk, the more pigment pouches you earn.

Step 2

Earn tiles

Open pouches to roll coloured tiles from this season's palette. Common, rare, and legendary colours mix into the mural.

Step 3

Paint together

Place tiles on your team's canvas to match the target pixel. Fill the mural before the other team to claim the season.

What makes it differentBuilt for people who actually walk.

  • Activity-only The only way to progress is to walk. No daily logins, no stamina timers, no XP shortcuts. Your steps are the currency.
  • No spending No real-money purchases. Every colour, every badge, every progress beat is earned. The game does not sell power.
  • Pseudonymous Play without an account. A stable player ID is all you need. Email and password are optional — only for backup across devices.
  • Steps-only We only read step counts. Not heart rate, not workouts, not sleep, not weight. The Health Connect permission is one toggle, scoped narrowly.
  • Permanent record Each season's mural lives on. Past murals are preserved as a collective artwork; your contribution becomes a badge that travels with your account.
  • Independent Made by one person. Stridekeep is built by a solo developer in the Netherlands. No publisher, no ad networks, no third-party trackers.
