Delete your Stridekeep account

Last updated: May 4, 2026.

Stridekeep gives you two ways to delete your account and the data tied to it. The in-app path is instant and self-serve; this web page is for players who have already uninstalled the app and can no longer reach the in-app button.

If you still have the app installed

Use the in-app self-serve deletion — it's instant and irreversible:

  1. Open Stridekeep.
  2. Tap the gear icon at the top of any screen.
  3. Tap Privacy.
  4. Tap DELETE MY ACCOUNT and confirm twice.

That deletes your account, badges, lifetime stats, and links your tile placements to a NULL author so they remain on the canvas anonymously. You'll be returned to a fresh anonymous player on the same device.

If you've already uninstalled

Fill the form below. Submitting opens your mail app with a pre-filled message to privacy@stridekeep.app. Your request will be processed within 30 days, in line with our Privacy Policy.
